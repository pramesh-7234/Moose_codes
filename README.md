# Moose_codes: 
Some codes i wrote to simulate stuff on MOOSE. Just trying to get the hang of it

CHINTA PROJECTS - Contains the Jupyter Notebooks on the three simulations carried out on models to understand propagation of signals along a cylindrical dendrite and validate Rall's Hypothesis

CA1 Pyramidal - A project to prove and validate the presence of A type Potassium Channels in distal regions of dendrites in hippocampal CA1 Pyramidal Neurons which prevent Action Potentials from back propagating into the denrites.
